# SOF-014 — Guided first quest and Bug Squash

## Builder request

Start working through the prioritized product improvements and build them to a high quality bar.

## Implemented interpretation

Begin with the highest-impact product slice: make the first visit understandable and complete one honest end-to-end booth quest before adding sponsor or public-fuel behavior.

Acceptance behavior:

- Ember explains Fire City and “Fire is earned, never bought” in two short steps.
- The HUD points the player toward Maya at Kindred Labs.
- Maya explains a product-shaped problem and launches Bug Squash.
- Bug Squash lasts 30 seconds, requires eight hits, supports pointer and keyboard input, and has success, failure, retry, and leave paths.
- Success returns the player near Maya and awards an explicitly local Practice Spark.
- Throwing the Practice Spark visibly grows Kindred’s fire without changing or claiming a public score.
- Existing uncommitted asset work is preserved.
- Tests, lint, typecheck, asset validation, build, and browser walkthrough are used as evidence.
